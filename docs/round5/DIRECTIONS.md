# Round 5, two design directions

Decision, 24 September 2026: Gauthier chose Direction A, with a subtle glass edge around the main calls to action. A is now the default. Direction B and the switch were removed in Milestone 7. This file records what was compared.

## What both directions share

* Seven type sizes: 44, 32, 22, 17, 15, 13 and 12 (11 in B). Each has a fixed line height.
* One 4px spacing scale, one button family (primary, secondary, quiet, in 28, 36 and 44 heights) and one window chrome.
* No brackets, no LIVE label, no HUD ticks. Window titles use the wordmark style. Close and maximise sit on the right.
* Bubbles shrink to their text, up to 75 percent on desktop and 85 percent on mobile. Grouped bubbles share one X mark and change corner shape along the run.
* The first beat rests at the foot of the window under a large X mark, the way a messaging app opens a conversation. No gap is left below it.
* Product and Portal cards sit inside the conversation as attachments.
* Figures are set large in Archivo with tabular numerals. Mono is kept for wallet addresses.
* Focus rings show for keyboard users only.
* On mobile the dock becomes a solid bottom bar and the chat ends above it, so the dock never covers a message. The review bar shrinks to its screen picker.
* A generated wallpaper replaces the round 2 photograph, which shows Apple logos.

## A, Calm

Soft radius (pill buttons, 14px cards, 16px windows), tonal surfaces and soft layered shadows. The primary button is solid white. Violet is kept for progress, selection and focus. The chamfer survives only on the X mark tile. Success, warning and error fold into the system as small dots beside neutral text, never as coloured outlines or coloured words.

## B, Precise

Square geometry. One chamfer shape, cut on the top right and bottom left corners, in two sizes: 6px on controls and 12 to 16px on cards and windows. The primary button is solid violet with dark text. Small data labels use uppercase mono. Semantic colours are removed. Status reads from the accent dot, ink weight and shape, and only errors keep a colour.

## Recommendation

A. The people this site has to convince are holders and investors, and for them trust comes from calm. A looks like a well run financial product. B is more distinctive and closer to today's identity. It also reads as more technical and more crypto native, which suits builders better than investors.
